SubVoice - Thong bao ban quyen thu vien ben thu ba
====================================================

Phan mem nay dong goi kem cac tep chay (libvlc.dll, libvlccore.dll va thu muc
plugins/) tu du an VLC media player, phat trien boi VideoLAN.

- libvlc / libvlccore duoc phat hanh theo giay phep GNU Lesser General
  Public License, phien ban 2.1 tro len (LGPL v2.1+).
- Mot so plugin cua VLC duoc phat hanh theo GNU General Public License (GPL).
- SubVoice CHI su dung cac tep nay o dang nhi phan (dynamic linking) qua
  python-vlc, khong sua doi ma nguon cua VLC.

Ban co quyen:
- Tu thay the cac tep libvlc.dll / libvlccore.dll / plugins/ trong thu muc
  cai dat SubVoice bang phien ban khac tuong thich, theo dieu khoan LGPL.
- Tai ma nguon day du cua VLC tai: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc
- Tai VLC media player day du (ban doc lap, khong phu thuoc SubVoice) tai:
  https://www.videolan.org/vlc/

Toan van giay phep LGPL v2.1: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
Toan van giay phep GPL v2: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html

VLC va VideoLAN la thuong hieu cua VideoLAN non-profit organization.
SubVoice khong lien ket chinh thuc voi VideoLAN.
